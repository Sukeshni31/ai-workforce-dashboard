# AI Workforce Transition Dashboard

A Google Sheet-powered dashboard that tracks workforce AI transition readiness at the employee level — with KPI cards, filters, risk banding, and drill-down detail views.

---

## What it does

HR leaders and GCC heads can load their workforce data from a published Google Sheet CSV and instantly get:

- **KPI summary cards** — snapshot of workforce AI transition status
- **Risk band filters** — Low (≤2), Mid (=3), High (≥4)
- **Department and recommendation filters**
- **Employee search** by name, role, skills, domain, or ID
- **Drill-down side panel** — click any employee row for full detail view

---

## Use case

Designed for HR teams managing AI-driven workforce transformation — identifying which employees need reskilling, which roles are at risk, and where to prioritise capability investment.

Particularly relevant for GCCs and Global Development Centres planning AI adoption roadmaps.

---

## How to use

1. Publish your workforce Google Sheet as a CSV
2. Update the CSV URL in `app.js`
3. Open `index.html` in a browser or deploy to any static host
